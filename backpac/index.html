BackPac: DEI Calendar

A dashboard for ERG managers to plan and track group activities

Overview

BackPac, a 2B SaaS company, helps create inclusive workplaces and empower employees. They support Employee Resource Groups with tools and resources.

I led the design of a new dashboard for efficient campaign management.

Impact

The new feature helped secure new clients, including Figma, and improved the engagement rate by 43%.

Timeline & Status

1 month, launched in Jan. 2023

Team

1 product designer, 1 product manager, 2 developers, 1QA

My Contribution

Sole designer for the project.

Pain point analysis, UX/UI design, user testing, design review for the dev

TAKING INSPIRATION FROM COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

3.1 EXAMPLES OF BLOCK CALENDARS

Intuitive

The layout's intuitiveness lets users navigate easily, thanks to their existing mental model.

Easy to display information layers

This layout assists in displaying multiple data layers, aiding users in understanding complexity.

2B vs 2C difference?

DESIGN V1.0

DESIGN V1.0

I tried “block calendar” in V1.0, but found it’s not the most efficient and effective way to display information.

Cons of V1

Waste of space

Since there are only 3-4 campaigns each quarter, the block layout would appear very empty and waste space in practical use.

Hard to show details

It is difficult to display detailed campaign information such as status, date, and full name on the initial screen using a block layout.

3.4 Comparison between V1.0 and V2.0

DESIGN V2.0

DESIGN V1.0

WHY V2.0 —> V3.0?

WHY V2.0 —> V3.0?

I conducted testing (n=3) and sought feedback from the dev team and PM.

From Usability Testing

Hard to know which activities require actions

There are three types of activities in the system. Users prefer a more visual and straightforward way to separate them

Hard to know which activities require actions

Users refer clear ways to inform them which activities need approval, review, and editing.

From PM

What if users want to plan activities for many months ahead?

For some inactive ERG managers, they prefer to plan activities 1 quarter ahead. A flexible way for them to view the dates are necessary.

From the Dev Team

Don’t use drag interaction to add activities.

Given the existing checkbox component and the rarity of drag interactions on desktop interfaces, using checkboxes for adding activities is preferable.

Since we had tight timeline to deliver this feature, using old components can help the dev team save time, I agreed to use the old checkbox as the adding interaction. The PM’s words inspired to think about more potential use cases. And I incorporated the user feedback in the V3.0.

3.6 Business changes and its impact on design

DESIGN V4.0

DESIGN V4.0

I changed the design to a vertical layout to ensure it is sustainable and scalable.

Iteration changes

I modified the layout from a horizontal to a vertical one. This modification not only meets the current user needs but also aligns with the near-future plans. It has two major benefits:

Display full names to avoid misunderstanding for HR

From the HR perspective of the dashboard, HR can view activities of all Employee Resource Groups. Limited space displaying only short names may lead to confusion for HR.

Easier to add more suggested activities

Future tiered pricing allows clients on the advanced plan to choose over five campaigns quarterly. The horizontal layout displays more items and offers future flexibility.
