Flickplay: Community Challenge

Overview

Flickplay Inc. is paving the path to become the destination for fans to buy game-ready digital toys and discover communities, leveraging the platform’s mix-reality capability.

In Dec. 2023, I redesigned the community challenge with an MVP approach to boost user engagement.

Timeline & Status

3 weeks, in dev process, establish to be launch in early 2024

Team

1 Product designer, 1 iOS developer, 1 community manager

My Contribution

UX, UI, visual design, & project management

TL; DR

TL; DR

Problem

Community challenges are one of the key user experiences in Flickplay. For users, these challenges offer the opportunity to create videos featuring their favorite characters and win rewards. For businesses, community challenges drive purchasing conversion and keep users active.

However, despite significant efforts in marketing and community management, the challenges currently suffer from a low engagement rate.

Solution

The redesign aims to encourage users to be more active in participating community challenges . I focused on improving the community challenge details page to make it more scannable and appealing, ensuring users can quickly learn about the challenge winners, and enhancing the video posting flow to prevent users from forgetting hashtags.

CONTEXT

Flickplay lies at the intersection of e-commerce, social media, and IP entertainment.

It offers a playful collecting and social interaction experience for IP fans, collectors, and Gen Z. Users can buy mystery blind boxes, unlock digital characters, and interact with them through AR videos, shuffle games, conversations, community engagement, and more.

IP entertainment is a supply-driven market. IP companies, Flickplay, and users are the main players in the eco-system. Flickplay licenses IP from partners and provide exciting entertainment experience for users

INSIGHTS

I sythesized 4 key user insights from the research.

Poor scan-ability of challenge details

Currently, all information, including rules and prompts, is within a single paragraph due to CMS limitations. Users find it challenging to digest this information

Forget to create and post videos with hashtags to join challenges

Users must include challenge hashtags when posting videos, but they often forget. Consequently, their videos don't count towards the challenge.

Users cannot learn the results in App in time

Infrastructure limitations lead to results being announced on social media, except for the App, causing confusion and reducing participation motivation.

Users cannot track the progress of multi-prompt challenges

We are currently running multi-prompt challenges where users need to fulfill multiple prompts by posting multiple videos. However, users cannot see their past submissions in one place.

5.5 New user flow (v1)

Questions about V1

After the idea was confirmed, I delved deeper into the design of the challenge page for users who have joined the challenge. Considering both business metrics and user needs, I approached the V2 design with the following three questions in mind.

How to promote sales of digital toys?

Selling digital toys is one of the key revenue streams and business metrics for the product. We want to encourage purchases.

How to boost more video creation after the user joined the challenge?

We allow users to upload multiple videos for a single challenge. In V1, it was confusing for users to understand that they were allowed to submit multiple videos.

How to help users find videos they created for the challenge?

Currently, users can find their videos in their profiles, but they would prefer a dedicated place to find recent submissions for the challenge.

Impact

The redesign goes beyond boosting user engagement; it sparks team focus on the community and inspires new community-centric features.

Success metrics

Satisfaction +66.7%

Direct metrics will be tracked:

Participants and videos per challenge/Accept challenge rate/challenge interactions (views likes comments)

Indirect metrics will be tracked:

DAU/WAU/mystery box sales data

Pave the way for the 2024 strategy

Aligns with partners'm interest

It strategically aligns with and attract more business partners that are mainly gaming and IP companies who are interested in connecting with their fans in innovative ways.

Community becomes a primary tab

This initial step in re-designing the community section elevates the team's focus on community. Consequently, the product team is committing additional resources to the community section and making it a primary tab in the 2024.

Inspire more community-focused features

As a follow-up of the design, the product team is crafting two pivotal features, quests and the lucky spin, to further activate the community.
