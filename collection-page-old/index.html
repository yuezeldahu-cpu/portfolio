Flickplay: Marketplace Collection Page

A collection introduction to help visitors understand the product better

Overview

During my time at Flickplay, I helped launch the website marketplace and iterate the design to improve conversion funnel.


I designed the IP collection page to educate new users about the new product concept "digital mystery box".

Impact

Designed 3 webpages and their relevant flows, built scalable templates for different IPs, and improve NPS from -50% to 33%

Timeline & Status

March 2024

Team

1 Product Designer(Me)

1 Content Manager

1 Front-end Engineer

1 3D Artist

1 Animator

My Contribution

I led the design of this project, from user research till testing, working closely with a cross-functional team to ensure seamless execution.

TL; DR

TL; DR

Problem

In this project, I aim to further improve the conversation funnel to increase mystery box sales and the App download rate on the web marketplace.

Through data analysis and user interviews, I found that users lack a clear understanding of Flickplay's offerings and the experience they can expect after making a purchase, which hinders them from purchasing.

Solution

I designed highly interactive introduction pages for different IP collections to educate users about new product concepts. The final design includes responsive layouts for desktop, mobile, and tablet views across three IP pages.
