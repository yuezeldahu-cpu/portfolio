360 Drum Performance

360 immersive VR music video where players mix drum performance

Overview

To explore the potential of spatial in VR, we created a 360 musical video where users can mix and adjust the performance of four drummers arond.

My Contribution

Modeling, Interaction design, UI design, Environment design, VFX design. I prototyped these things in Unity, helped with spatial audio setup, and integrated all parts together.

Timeline & Status

Nov. 2022 (1 weeks)

Tool

Unity, Blender, Visual Studio, Resonance Audio Plugin

Team

Laine Fleszar, Tanner Rodriguez, Alexa Kenny, Zelda Hu

DESIGN BREAK DOWN

Concept

When brainstorming the idea, we consider the possibilities of VR from two perspectives, visual and audio.


For the audio part, we would like our users to be the mixer of the performance and allow them to play with different filters and effects. And we decided on three basic features (mute, solo, and volume) and four filters (high pass filter, low pass filter, reverb, and chorus).


For the visual part, I want to add a starting scene and ending scene to enhance the narrative of the concert besides implementing UI to the performance scene. Four colors of red , orange, green and blue are the main visual theme of the concert and I also applied them while designing the starting and ending scene.

Visual Design

I am fully responsible for the visual design of the project. I think it is kind of sudden and abrupt to jump into the performance directly and believe it is important to set up the vibe and mental expectation of audiences before they enter the show. For the design of the beginning scene, I brainstormed two ideas.


In the first idea (the top one on the sketch), the audience will stand on a floating platform and there will be different drums rotating round him/her. The audience can grab the ticket flying from a distance to enter the show. In the second idea (the bottom one on the sketch), the audience will stand in a tunnel and a glowing ball in a mix of red, orange, blue, and green will guide the audience walk to the further of the tunnel. The audience will be transported to the show via space warp.


Finally, our team decided on going with the second idea. I think the second idea provides a contrast between the introduction environment and the performance scene. And the warp is a more unique and intuitive way for scene transition.


I modeled the starting scene and the VFX effects.

Sound Design

Our team brainstormed many ways that allow users to be the mixer of the performance. Finally, we agreed on creating four channels for each drum player and allowing users to manipulate the volume, simple EQ filters, and mute, solo, and reverb each channel. Using the resonance audio plugin, we placed four channels of sound in corresponding positions and create a sense of spatial audio for a player.

Reflection

What I could have done different…

More interaction if I have time

The environment and the content of the concert is relatively simple. To enhance the audience experience, I would like to introduce a strong narration to the concert. I achieve my goal to some extent through the starting scene, but I believe there is space for improvement and we can be more creative if we have some more time. For example, is there a way to gamify the concert? How could I change the environment of the concert to tell a story of drum history? There are many possibilities worth exploring.

Better workflow & timeline management

In the future collaboration, I will highlight the need for early testing in VR and encourage people to raise problems at any time, rather than waiting until the end. Unlike UX design or product design, VR design requires us to consider more technical feasibility and dealing with technical errors by ourselves. We cannot connect everyone’s parts together directly. Therefore, communication plays an even more important role.
